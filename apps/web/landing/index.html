The problem

Millions of food vendors are invisible online

Street and neighbourhood food stalls feed Latin American cities, yet most take orders by WhatsApp: no discovery, no order queue, no status. Big delivery apps are built for formal restaurants, with tax registration and percentage commissions.

Big apps weren’t built for them

  • Tax registration required (RUT / RFC)
  • Percentage commission on every order
  • WhatsApp: no discovery, no queue, no status

Official statistics describe the size of the informal economy; they are not Chuzos’ addressable market.

Business model

Our paying customer is the stall

Chuzos lets street food stalls take app orders for a flat fee instead of a commission. Stalls prepay credits; every order debits COP 1,200. Food money goes straight from the customer to the stall. A tax ID is optional to start.

Roadmap

One corridor first, then the next city

  1. 1

    Chapinero pilot

    90 days in one walkable Bogotá corridor. The key question: after 20 free orders, do stalls pay to top up?

  2. 2

    More Bogotá corridors

    Expand corridor by corridor only if the pilot hits its go signal (≥40% of stalls top up, customers come back).

  3. 3

    Mexico City (CDMX)

    Same model in pesos mexicanos: MXN 5 per order. The prototype already runs MXN flows.
